Frequently asked

Clear answers before you connect a channel.

Can Atlas send email, SMS or WhatsApp messages today?

This source release contains governed message and workflow contracts, not connected delivery providers. Delivery requires a tenant's provider connection, verified consent/suppression data and a production worker.

Can an AI agent change company records by itself?

Atlas limits agent tools to the tenant's approved capabilities. Sensitive writes must use tenant-bound approval evidence; the platform owner identity does not flow from a tenant request.

Is the Atlas dashboard a live SaaS account?

No. The included command center is a sample-data preview. Signup, authentication, checkout and connected inboxes require production services that are outside this source release.

What does an AI customer operations platform do?

It connects customer conversations, service work, follow-up and automation into a shared workflow so teams can respond consistently while keeping people in control of sensitive decisions.

Which teams is Atlas designed for?

Atlas is designed around service businesses that need lead response, customer service, appointment follow-up and operational handoffs to stay coordinated across a growing team.
